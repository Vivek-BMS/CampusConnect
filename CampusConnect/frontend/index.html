How CampusConnect Works

Seamless 4-step lifecycle ensuring high verification integrity and zero false handovers.

📝

1. Report Belongings

Submit details of lost or found articles with photos, location, and identifying marks.

🧠

2. Smart Matching

Our Java algorithm cross-checks title, category, color, location, and date to spot matches.

🛡️

3. Verify Ownership

Submit private ownership proof only visible to campus safety staff for verification.

🤝

4. Physical Handover

Collect item securely at campus security desk with digital handover acknowledgement.

ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE

Platform Features Built for Campus Scale

Engineered with Spring Security, role-based protection, and zero public data leaks.

⚖️ Weighted Similarity Engine

Evaluates Category (20%), Title Levenshtein (25%), Color (15%), Brand (10%), Location (15%), Date gap (10%), and description keywords (5%).

🔒 Private Ownership Vault

Secret serial numbers, laptop passwords, and inside contents are concealed from public browse and strictly visible to authorized verifiers.

📱 Digital QR Inventory Labels

Automatic QR code generator for found items allows the campus security desk to tag locker bins and scan directly for verification.

🔔 In-App Reactive Notifications

Instant alerts whenever a high-confidence match is detected, claims are submitted, or handovers are approved.

📊 Chart.js Recovery Analytics

Monthly resolution trends, category breakdowns, and audit trails help campus safety monitor recovery rates.

🌐 English & Kannada Bilingualism

Full support for local language accessibility across student dashboards and report submission forms.
